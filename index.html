FAQ

Aksar pooche gaye sawal

How do I order?

Choose a plan, tap its button and fill in the order form. You receive an order ID and payment instructions. Your order is activated only after we verify your payment.

Is the payment one-time?

Yes. Each package is a one-time payment with lifetime access to the purchased software plan. Monthly AI credits are included per package, and additional credits may require separate payment.

Which plan should I choose?

Starter covers voice conversation and basic assistance. Professional adds AI Office, multiple AI agents, file handling and WhatsApp integration. Ultimate adds advanced automation and premium support.

Kaunsi languages chalti hain?

Hindi, Urdu aur English, sab plans mein.

How is my order information used?

Your name, email and WhatsApp number are used only to process your order and contact you about it.
